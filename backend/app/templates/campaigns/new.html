Step 9 of 9

Launch Verification & Dispatch Lifecycle

What happens once you click "Create Campaign Draft".

1. IMMUTABLE SNAPSHOT

The matching audience leads are frozen into an immutable campaign recipient list. Subsequent CRM lead edits will not alter the launched campaign.

2. PRE-DISPATCH VALIDATION

On the Campaign Details page, click "Validate" to execute final provider handshake checks, verify suppression entries, and inspect tokens.

3. RATE-LIMITED DISPATCH

Click "Launch" to dispatch. Messages are queued through the Celery/Redis background worker pipeline with exponential backoff and reply tracking.
